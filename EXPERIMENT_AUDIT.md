## Overall Verdict: WARN

The eight current HDF5 datasets pass exhaustive integrity and provenance
checks. The WARN is not a data failure: training and evaluation have not run,
the implementation is an official-core GC/offline adaptation rather than an
official benchmark reproduction, and the planned evaluation is a two-task,
single-seed pilot.

## Checks

### A. Goal provenance: PASS

Goals are final images from terminal-derived, episode-safe dataset segments;
they are never generated by the model. See
`scripts/data/convert_ogbench_npz_tdmpc2.py` and
`scripts/train/tdmpc2_official_gc.py`.

### B. Reward, terminal, and action alignment: PASS

For every segment, reward `+1` occurs only on the transition entering the goal,
terminal is true only on the final goal observation, the final dummy action is
zero, and the horizon-3 sampler cannot select that dummy action. Official
TD-MPC2 consumes the shifted terminal label as `(next_z, reward, terminated)`.

### C. Dataset integrity: PASS

Every one of 12,240,000 logical pixel rows across all eight HDF5 files was
decoded and compared exactly to its reconstructed source-NPZ row: zero pixel
mismatches. All stored non-goal actions exactly matched their NPZ source;
actions were finite and in `[-1, 1]`. Episode offsets, lengths, source episode
IDs, source steps, rewards, terminals, dtypes, and shapes were exact.

### D. Official-core fidelity: WARN

The TD-MPC2 submodule is clean at
`e9f59321933cbc8e11a002b842adc7d4ffae8ff1`; model update, targets,
optimizers, RunningScale, and planner are unmodified. The custom 6-channel
current/goal input, HDF5 replay, episodic labels, 100k budget, and seed 1 make
this an official-core adaptation, not an official benchmark reproduction.

### E. Evaluation integrity: WARN (`simulation_only`)

The evaluator uses MuJoCo simulator success directly, without self-normalizing
scores or model-generated ground truth. Scope is currently only two
single-cube checkpoints, 10 episodes each, one training seed, and one
evaluation seed; it must be described as a pilot.

### F. Result existence: PASS as a negative check

At audit time no checkpoints or evaluation `results.json` existed. No
performance result is claimed. GPU smoke and training remain pending GPU
availability.
